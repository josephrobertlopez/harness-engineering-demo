# Help centre FAQ

The five topics that make up ~40% of tier-1 tickets. Each entry is a
question, a `keywords:` line, and the answer support has signed off.

## How do I reset my password?

keywords: reset, forgot, forgotten, locked, sign-in, login

Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. We email a reset link that is
valid for 30 minutes. If it does not arrive, check spam, then contact
support.

## How do I change my billing plan?

keywords: plan, upgrade, downgrade, billing, subscription

Go to **Settings → Billing → Change plan**. Upgrades take effect
immediately and are pro-rated. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal.

## How do I get a refund?

keywords: refund, money, charged, charge

Refunds are available within 14 days of a charge. Open **Settings →
Billing → History**, choose the charge, and select **Request refund**.

## How do I export my data?

keywords: export, download, csv, backup

**Settings → Data → Export** produces a CSV of all your records. Large
accounts receive an email link when the export is ready.

## How do I delete my account?

keywords: delete, close, cancel, remove

**Settings → Account → Delete account**. Deletion is permanent after 7
days; sign in during that window to undo it.
